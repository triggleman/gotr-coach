# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (first Plugin Hub release)

- Step-by-step guidance for Mass, Team and Solo, following the OSRS Wiki strategies
- Guide line that follows the game's own walking route, ends on the target, dashed over the rubble shortcut
- Altar picks (even points, balance, best cell, most XP, single type), including portal talismans and altars still open while the round closes
- Portal first (in red), an on-screen portal countdown, and no altar trips right before a portal
- Timeline bar with labelled sections and countdowns, plus a fragments/points goal bar
- Energy-cap tracking (1,000 per type, 1,200 total, 1,320 with a yew lantern)
- Ready check: pickaxe, chisel and uncharged cells
- Fragments-reached banner and inventory flash
- Side panel: next step, round, points, quick settings (incl. opening fragments), session stats, last round, personal bests, history
- A grade and tips after every round, XP per round, personal bests
- Rune icons on altars, menu clean-up, notifications
